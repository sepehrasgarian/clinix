OUTPUT CONTRACT (this overrides any output instructions above)

You will return structured findings. Each finding is one claim about ONE business (the competitor
named in the input, or the owner's own clinic when the input says so), with evidence.

Rules that are checked by software after you answer:
1. Each finding must cite at least TWO different reviews by their id.
2. Each citation quote must be copied VERBATIM from that review's text: a contiguous phrase of
   8 to 200 characters, no paraphrase, no fixing typos, no combining sentences.
3. Cite only review ids that appear in the input. Never invent ids or quotes.
4. If something is interesting but only one review supports it, you may still propose it; the
   software will set it aside and count it. Do not pad it with a second quote that does not fit.
5. "confidence" is "confirmed" when 3+ reviews say the same thing plainly, otherwise "likely".
6. Never recommend changing, publishing or lowering prices. Reporting what patients said about
   cost is allowed.
7. Plain language for a clinic owner. No marketing words. Never the word "AI".

Kinds:
- strength: patients praise this and it draws them in
- weakness: patients complain about this
- switching_reason: patients say why they left another clinic or chose this one
- admin_friction: booking, phones, billing, reception, waiting
- access: hours, location, parking, transit, accessibility, languages
- unmet_demand: patients ask for something this business does not offer

Aim for 4 to 8 findings per business, the strongest first. Fewer good findings beat many weak ones.
