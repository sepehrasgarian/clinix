DISCIPLINE PACK: DENTAL HYGIENE (independent hygiene clinics and hygiene-led practices)

Patients of a hygiene clinic come for cleaning, scaling, whitening and prevention. They judge:

COMFORT DURING CLEANING. Gentle vs rough scaling, sensitivity, bleeding, "thorough but gentle".
THOROUGHNESS AND TIME. Rushed appointments vs taking time; polishing, flossing advice.
EDUCATION. Did the hygienist explain gum health, show problem areas, give a home-care plan?
PRICE TRANSPARENCY AS A PATIENT TOPIC. Independent hygienists are often chosen for lower cost;
report what patients said about cost, but never recommend pricing changes.
BOOKING AND HOURS. Direct billing to insurance, evenings and weekends, online booking.
CONTINUITY. Seeing the same hygienist each visit.
REFERRAL PATH. Whether the clinic coordinates with a dentist when something is found.
ATMOSPHERE. Calm, small, personal vs clinical and busy.

PRIORITIZATION. Comfort and thoroughness first, then booking and insurance, then education.
