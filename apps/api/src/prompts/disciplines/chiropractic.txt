DISCIPLINE PACK: CHIROPRACTIC

RELIEF AND RESULTS. Immediate relief, lasting improvement, or "no change". Quote outcomes.
TRUST AND PRESSURE. Long prepaid plans, pressure to commit to many visits, x-rays pushed,
"only did what I needed". This is the main trust issue in the field.
TECHNIQUE AND COMFORT. Gentle vs forceful adjustments, instrument-assisted, fear of cracking.
EXPLANATION. Did the chiropractor explain findings and the plan?
TIME PER VISIT. Five-minute adjustments vs thorough sessions.
SPECIFIC CONDITIONS: back, neck, headaches, sciatica, pregnancy, athletes, kids.
BOOKING AND HOURS. Walk-ins, same-day, evenings, weekends.
INSURANCE. Direct billing, extended health.
ADDED SERVICES. Massage, physiotherapy, acupuncture, custom orthotics.

PRIORITIZATION. Trust and pressure first, then results, then comfort of technique, then booking.
