DISCIPLINE PACK: PHYSIOTHERAPY

Patients judge physiotherapy by whether they got better and whether they felt looked after.

RESULTS. "Pain gone", "back to running", "no improvement after 10 sessions". Quote the outcome.
ONE-ON-ONE TIME. Hands-on treatment vs being left on a machine or with an assistant; rushed
sessions; how many patients the therapist sees at once.
EXPLANATION AND HOME PROGRAM. Did they explain the cause, give exercises, follow up?
SPECIFIC CONDITIONS patients name: back, neck, knee, shoulder, post-surgery, sports injury,
concussion, pelvic health, vestibular. Note where a clinic is known.
INSURANCE AND BILLING. Direct billing, extended health, MVA/WSIB handling, receipts.
BOOKING AND HOURS. Same-week appointments, evenings, weekends, cancellations.
CONTINUITY. Same therapist each visit.
FACILITIES. Gym space, equipment, cleanliness, parking.
ADDED SERVICES. Massage, chiropractic, acupuncture, shockwave under the same roof.

Switching reasons: "left my old clinic because…", "finally someone who listened".

PRIORITIZATION. Results and one-on-one time first, then explanation, then booking and insurance.
